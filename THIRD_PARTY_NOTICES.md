## ECMA-376 DrawingML preset shape definitions

- Source: ECMA-376, 1st edition, `OfficeOpenXML-DrawingMLGeometries.zip`
- Official download: https://ecma-international.org/publications-and-standards/standards/ecma-376/
- Bundled file: `assets/presetShapeDefinitions.xml`

The geometry definitions are used to evaluate the standard DrawingML preset
shape formulas stored by PowerPoint-compatible `.pptx` files.
